The Ecosystem & Ground Reality

The 1-Hectare Forest Coffee Sanctuary

Impact Plus Coffee is managed directly on the ground by Tika General Trading, cultivating Arabica beneath indigenous broadleaf trees in the natural Ethiopian highlands.

Authentic Agroforestry Canopy

We never monocrop. Coffee trees are planted inside native forest systems under high-altitude shade trees that preserve soil moisture, protect native birds, and prevent land erosion.

Operating Entity: Tika General Trading

Tika General Trading directly manages local employment, long-term leased land compliance, irrigation channels, and dedicated organic seedling nurseries.

GPS Plot Coordinates

Every partner block is tagged with GPS coordinates and photographic documentation so your company can trace the exact section of Ethiopian forest you are restoring.
