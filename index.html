The Origin & Ground Reality

The 1-Hectare Arabica Sanctuary

Managed by Tika General Trading, our project bridges European corporate stewardship with authentic Ethiopian agricultural traditions.

Heritage Arabica Cultivar

Ethiopia is the birthplace of Arabica. Our trees are grown in optimal high-altitude soils, utilizing shade-grown techniques that protect native biodiversity.

Managed Leased Land Structure

Operating under transparent land leases, Tika General Trading oversees everyday agronomical care, labor standards, irrigation, and security on site.

Uncompromising Honesty
